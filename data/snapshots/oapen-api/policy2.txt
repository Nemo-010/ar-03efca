 Book Analytics Service 
 Book Analytics Service 
 The Book Analytics Service is operated by OAPEN, and forms part of our open access books community infrastructure. 
 The Book Analytics Service aggregates standardised usage data from a range of sources including OAPEN, Google Books, Crossref Event Data, and JSTOR, and provides publishers with a dashboard overview. View the demo dashboard . 
 How the dashboard can help you 
 The dashboard is easy to share with colleagues and authors. Use it to help you: 
 understand usage, and how this varies by platform, subject, country, and titles 
 easily synthesise and consolidate data across a wide range of platforms, and save time on manual data collection 
 inform publishing decisions 
 explain the value of open access publishing to authors and funders. 
 “Implementing the dashboard for Big Ten Open Books has been essential to our project’s success and has helped the broader academic community to realize how impactful Humanities scholarship can be when made open access.” - Kate McCready, Big Ten Academic Alliance

 Background to the Book Analytics Dashboard project 
 Funded by the Mellon Foundation , the Book Analytics Dashboard project (2022-2025) is focused on creating a sustainable OA book focused analytics service. Partners include Curtin University, Educopia, The Mellon Foundation, and OAPEN.

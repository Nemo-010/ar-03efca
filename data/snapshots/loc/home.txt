 Free to Use and Reuse: Work in America 
 These images highlight collections generated by the American Folklife Center's field survey projects between 1977 and 1998. In urban and rural communities—and in traditional occupations, small businesses, factories, and natural resource management—you see these photographs a wide range of people engaged in jobs that contribute to economies, individual identities, and cultural communities.

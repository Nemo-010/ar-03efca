Home | Thoth Skip to main content About Services Metadata Pricing Docs Login Open infrastructure for publishing Thoth Thoth is a non-profit, open infrastructure platform for publishing, bringing together metadata management, dissemination, hosting, and usage insights in one place. Built for open access books and chapters, Thoth helps publishers, libraries, and platforms manage their metadata once, distribute it widely, host catalogues and websites, and understand how their content is used. Manage metadata for books and chapters in a single, structured, open system. Distribute everywhere with automated exports and integrations across platforms, aggregators, and indexes. Host books and websites with fast, scalable infrastructure powered by your Thoth metadata. Understand usage through aggregated, privacy-respecting metrics and reporting. Register and preserve with DOI support, archiving, and long-term preservation workflows. Stay open with open APIs, no lock-in, and CC0-licensed metadata.

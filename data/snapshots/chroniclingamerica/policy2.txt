 Chronicling America
 Datasets and API access to Chronicling America
 The
 Chronicling America Historic American Newspapers collection
 provides access to millions of digitized newspaper pages produced by the
 National Digital Newspaper Program (NDNP) , a partnership between the National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH) and
 the Library of Congress. From 2007 to 2025, the legacy website hosting the
 Chronicling America collection had its own dedicated API. Beginning in 2025,
 the
 Chronicling America collection 
 is accessible exclusively via the loc.gov API. Visit the
 Guide to Chronicling America Newspaper Datasets and API Access 
 for more information.
